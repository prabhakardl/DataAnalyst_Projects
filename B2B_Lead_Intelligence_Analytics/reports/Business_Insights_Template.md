# Business Insights Report

## Executive summary
Use the Power BI results to identify the strongest countries, industries, technologies and campaign templates.

## Recommended analysis
- Prioritize countries with high qualified-lead volume AND high reply rate.
- Prioritize technologies with strong lead density and commercial relevance.
- Compare industries using reply rate and downstream opportunity conversion.
- Investigate high-score leads without responses for follow-up.
- Stop or redesign low-performing templates.

## Final recommendation
The dashboard should be used as a weekly business-development decision tool rather than only a historical report.
